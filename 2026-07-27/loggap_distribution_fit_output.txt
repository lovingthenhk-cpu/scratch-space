=== Interpretation ===
Gamma MLE shape=2.311: shape>1 confirms sub-exponential (more regular)
gaps, consistent with the sd/mean=0.62<1 finding from 2026-07-26. The
Gamma/Weibull fits both have lower KS D than the plain Exponential, and
(by AIC, which penalizes their extra free parameter) still come out ahead
of Exponential despite that penalty -- so this is not merely 'more
parameters always fit better'.
CAVEAT #1: n=70 gaps is a small sample; shape parameter estimates at this
size have wide confidence intervals (a bootstrap CI is the natural next
step once more records exist -- see PROGRESS.md priority 3).
CAVEAT #2: this whole analysis silently assumes the 70 gaps are i.i.d.
draws from a fixed distribution, which per 2026-07-26 item 1 is itself
in question (nearby-n correlation). A Gamma/Weibull fit to a NON-i.i.d.
sequence is still a valid empirical description, just not a proper
'stochastic model' in the strict sense.
